# Gemstone Expansion Plan

This document governs the expansion beyond the first five proof-run gemstones. Its purpose is to increase coverage without lowering source fidelity, scientific care, or structural consistency.

## Objective

Grow the collection from five pilot pages to 15-20 pages through small, diverse batches. Use the new cases to test the page contract, then deepen a small set of representative anchor pages after the contract has stabilized.

Expansion is successful only when breadth grows without hidden omissions, weaker validation, or one-off structure drift.

## Batch 1: Diversity Test

Batch 1 adds five gemstones chosen to exercise different scientific and editorial shapes:

| Gemstone | Dutch source name | File | Why it is included |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amber | Barnsteen | `gemstones/amber.md` | Organic fossil resin rather than a mineral species. |
| Diamond | Diamant | `gemstones/diamond.md` | Native element with a very simple formula and major historical depth. |
| Lapis lazuli | Lapis lazuli | `gemstones/lapis-lazuli.md` | Rock/material whose identity is a mixture rather than one mineral species. |
| Obsidian | Obsidiaan | `gemstones/obsidian.md` | Volcanic glass with trade varieties and no crystal system. |
| Turquoise | Turkoois | `gemstones/turquoise.md` | Hydrated phosphate mineral with composition and locality variation. |

Source URLs remain in the Dutch index and should be checked from there before writing. The five pages must not be created from secondary summaries alone.

## Required Per-Page Quality Floor

Every page must pass the existing gemstone-process checklist:

- the primary source is read in source order;
- headings, paragraphs, lists, properties, images, and source links are represented;
- the source composition is preserved exactly;
- the normalized composition is chemically equivalent;
- mineral species, variety, rock, glass, organic, or trade-name status is explicit;
- at least two reliable external sources validate critical scientific claims;
- disagreements and unavailable fields remain visible;
- therapeutic claims are source-attributed and not medical advice;
- every source therapeutic claim, instruction, warning, and qualifier is transferred or explicitly marked missing;
- every source-specific historical, symbolic, naming, trade, cultural, and “Did You Know” claim is transferred or explicitly marked missing;
- source disclaimers, responsibility statements, and professional-care guidance are preserved or explicitly recorded;
- explicit mental, emotional, and physical concepts are mapped to canonical graph notes with source-qualified relationships;
- every concept candidate is marked mapped, intentionally unmapped, or blocked in the coverage audit;
- the page follows the shared structure and uses observational prose;
- a final composition and source-traceability review is complete.

No page is complete because it is readable or fast to produce. It is complete only when the evidence and structure are auditable.

## Batch Workflow

1. Select the batch and record the intended diversity before writing.
2. Read each primary source fully and capture a raw extraction checkpoint.
3. Create the five pages using the current specification.
4. Validate each page independently against the per-page quality floor.
5. Compare all five new pages with at least two pilot pages.
6. Record omissions, conflicts, one-off structures, and source-quality concerns.
7. Change the specification or process only after the batch review.
8. If the contract changes, recheck at least one affected pilot page before the next batch.

The batch is not accepted as a whole until every page passes. A strong page does not compensate for a weak page.

## Batch Metrics

Track these measures in the batch audit:

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Pages passing the checklist on first review | 5 of 5, or every exception documented and repaired |
| Omitted source sections, facts, images, or links | 0 unresolved |
| Omitted source-specific context claims or disclaimers | 0 unresolved |
| Therapeutic concept relationships source-mapped | Every explicit mapped concept has a canonical note and source scope |
| Unresolved composition ambiguities | 0 |
| Pages with at least two reliable external sources | 5 of 5 |
| Silent one-off format deviations | 0 |
| Specification changes caused by the batch | Recorded explicitly |
| Rework required after audit | Recorded, not hidden |

Do not use pages-per-day as a quality target. Rework and unresolved evidence are more important than throughput.

## Stop-The-Line Conditions

Pause expansion and repair the process if any of these occur:

- a primary source cannot be accessed or fully read;
- content is being reconstructed from memory or weak secondary summaries;
- source composition is ambiguous and has not been resolved or clearly marked;
- facts, links, images, or source distinctions are being dropped to fit the template;
- historical, symbolic, naming, trade, or “Did You Know” details are being dropped because they seem non-essential;
- source disclaimers or responsibility statements are being omitted during rewriting;
- therapeutic claims lose source attribution;
- repeated exceptions require informal formatting choices;
- external validation sources are weak, circular, or insufficient;
- the page contract no longer fits a material type such as a rock, glass, organic resin, or mixture.

An inaccessible source is a blocked page, not permission to lower the quality floor. Record the blocker and resume when the source can be read.

## After Batch 2

Once the collection reaches 15 pages, select four to six anchor pages for deeper treatment. Anchor work may add richer mineralogy, locality evidence, terminology, historical context, and graph links, but it must not weaken the common page contract.
